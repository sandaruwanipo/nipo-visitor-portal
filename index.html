Primary Focus

Trademarks Division

🏷️

Protects brand names, logos, slogans, and distinctive signs. Rights are acquired through official registration under IP Act No. 36 of 2003.

Protection Highlights
  • Validity: 10 years from filing date
  • Renewal: Every 10 years indefinitely[cite: 1]
  • Classification: Nice Classes 1 to 45
Secondary Wing

Copyright & Related Rights

📜

Protects literary, artistic, musical works, and software automatically upon creation without mandatory registration. Voluntary deposits available.

Protection Rules
  • Duration: Author's Lifetime + 70 Years
  • Deposit: Voluntary proof of ownership
Regional Protection

Geographical Indications (GIs)

🌍

Protects goods originating from specific regions where quality is linked to geographical origin (e.g., Ceylon Tea, Ceylon Cinnamon).

National GIs
  • Ceylon Tea & Ceylon Cinnamon
  • Managed via Producer Associations
